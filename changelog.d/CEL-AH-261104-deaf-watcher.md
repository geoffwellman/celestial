### Fixed
- The omp/pi inbox hook no longer goes deaf when its `cel inbox watch --parent` watcher disappears without an exit event. A spawn the kernel refuses (EAGAIN in a fork storm, EMFILE, ENOENT) reports only `error`, never `exit` - in node and in bun - and the hook used to swallow it while still holding the dead child, so nothing ever restarted the watcher. Liveness is now the child process itself: its pid AND the kernel start time recorded at spawn (so a pid the kernel has handed to another child of the agent - a bash tool - never reads as the watcher, and its process group is never signalled), not a zombie, still this session's child. It is re-checked at every `agent_end` and on a slow idle check (`CEL_INBOX_WATCH_CHECK_MS`, 60s); a missing watcher is respawned and the mail that arrived meanwhile is counted and delivered. After `session_shutdown` the watcher stays down - a late `agent_end` from the turn that was ending is no proof - until a `session_start` or a new turn starting shows the session went on. A reviewer retirement in progress, or the process exiting, keeps it down too.
- The steward and `cel doctor` now catch a deaf agent: inbox hook in argv but no live `cel inbox watch --parent <pid>` child, for a process up longer than `CEL_INBOX_DEAF_GRACE` (180s). Doctor reports it as a failure; the steward applies the stripped-hook rule (restart only when idle with an empty composer, otherwise tell root once) after the deafness has lasted `CEL_STEWARD_DEAF_CONFIRM` (120s) across consecutive ticks, so a gap between the hook's own restarts is not mistaken for it; a sighting whose row vanishes is forgotten. Solo lanes (`cel run solo --lane`) are now checked too, and restarted as lanes.
