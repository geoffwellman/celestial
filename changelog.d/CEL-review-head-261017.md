### Changed
- `cel-fanout review` now REQUIRES `--head <full 40-char sha>`: the verdict binds to the commit the reviewer names, never the worker's local HEAD. An `approved` is recorded only when that SHA is the PR's current remote head on a PR the fleet owns (re-read under the ledger lock); records carry `head_source: explicit`. `land` (and the work board's `landing` stage) accept a ledger approval only when it is explicit and matches the PR head being merged; historical approvals without `head_source` no longer authorize a same-account merge until re-attested, and `status` shows them as `old`. Reviewer role, policy block and docs updated. Installed sessions keep the old CLI until this is deployed.
