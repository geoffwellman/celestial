### Fixed
- `cel inbox wait --check` no longer stands a starting waiter down. The check asks the waiter's lock by taking it for a moment, and a waiter that tried the lock in that moment said "already armed - nothing started" while no waiter ran, so an agy agent could go idle with no waiter to wake it. A check and a waiter's try now take turns on a gate file beside the lock, so behind the gate a held lock always means another waiter.
- A `cel inbox wait` whose mailbox tail ends now prints "stopped without mail ... its mailbox tail ended" and exits 3. Before, it exited 1 and printed nothing, because stopping the tail, which was already gone, failed under cel's `set -e`. Every kill of the tail now allows for it being gone.
- A `cel inbox wait` woken by a mailbox that does not parse now exits 0 and ends with its instruction to read the mail and start the waiter again. Before, the mail preview failed under `pipefail`, so it exited 1 after its first line.
- The agy test of one waiter per reader no longer fails without a message on a loaded runner. It stopped the waiter by killing the waiter's tail first, under the suite's `set -e`; that kill failed whenever the waiter had no child at that instant or had already exited on its own. The test now stops the waiter with TERM only, and polls `--check` (up to about 10 seconds) instead of sleeping a fixed time.
