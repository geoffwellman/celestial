### Fixed
- Reviewers and team members now start with a cleaned environment. Their launch line (Herdr pane and Rex launch script alike) ends in a scrub that unsets every inherited variable whose name looks like a credential (`*_TOKEN`, `*_API_KEY`, `*_KEY`, `*_SECRET`, `*_PASSWORD`, `AWS_*`, `CLOUDFLARE_*`, `OPENROUTER_*`, ...) before the runtime starts, so an owner's Cloudflare, AWS or other unrelated tokens from the launching shell or the workspace's `env.local` no longer reach them. Kept: what the launch line sets itself (`OMP_GATEWAY_TOKEN` and `CEL_SESSION_ID` when gateway-routed, the workspace account's `GH_TOKEN`) and, for a member on a direct profile, that provider's key. Product agents and core are unchanged; docs/architecture.md ("Launch environment by role") lists what each role gets and proposes the same for them.
