### Fixed
- The steward's ready/Todo ticket nudge now goes to the right owner. A ticket whose repo declares a lane (`repos[].lane`) goes to that lane. If the lane is not running, one note per ticket goes to the product's agent (if `auto`) or to root, saying how to start the lane. A ticket whose product is `auto` is routed as before. A ticket whose product is `manual` or `none` on this box (including through `workspace.local.yaml`) and has no owning lane is no longer nudged. Its open nudges, including ones sent before this change and ones sent to any recipient, are taken down once, with a line explaining why (`<product> is set to manual here; no agent owns its Todo tickets`).
