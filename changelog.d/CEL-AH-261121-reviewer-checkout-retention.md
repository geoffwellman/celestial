### Fixed
- Reviewer checkouts under `~/.local/state/cel/reviews` were never removed (270 on one box, ~15 GB): a finished reviewer's checkout is kept by design and nothing came back for it. `cel gc` now removes one when its PR is merged or closed more than `CEL_REVIEW_CHECKOUT_KEEP_DAYS` (default 3) days ago - asked of the repo's own forge, GitHub or Forgejo, the answer naming exactly that PR, its close time an ISO-8601 instant from 2000 on; anything unreadable is kept - no reviewer of the PR is recorded, no pane, process cwd or open file is in it, no retained Rex retirement packet cites it, and it holds no untracked or modified file or commit of its own (ignored build output does not count); a private clone must also prove it is the PR's (HEAD at or behind the PR head, or cloned from the repo). One that holds work of its own, or cannot prove it, is kept and named. Every one of these is asked again right before the remove, under the agent launch lock `cel run` holds while it starts a reviewer. A linked worktree goes by a plain `git worktree remove`; the verdicts and evidence under the workspace's `.cel` are untouched. At most `CEL_GC_REVIEW_CHECKOUT_CAP` (default 30) checkouts are asked of the forge per pass, oldest first, and the gc summary counts the checkouts removed and the space freed.
- `forge_pr_view` (and `cel forge pr view|list`) now report `closed_at` on both forges.
