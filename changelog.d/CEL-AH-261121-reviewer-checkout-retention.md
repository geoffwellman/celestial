### Fixed
- Reviewer checkouts under `~/.local/state/cel/reviews` were never removed (270 on one box, ~15 GB): a finished reviewer's checkout is kept by design and nothing came back for it. `cel gc` now removes one when its PR is merged or closed (asked of the repo's own forge, GitHub or Forgejo; unreadable is kept) more than `CEL_REVIEW_CHECKOUT_KEEP_DAYS` (default 3) days ago, no reviewer of the PR is recorded, no pane, process cwd or open file is in it, no retained Rex retirement packet cites it, and it holds no untracked or modified file (ignored build output does not count; one that holds work of its own is kept and named). A linked worktree goes by a plain `git worktree remove`; the verdicts and evidence under the workspace's `.cel` are untouched. At most `CEL_GC_REVIEW_CHECKOUT_CAP` (default 30) checkouts are asked of the forge per pass, oldest first, and the gc summary counts the checkouts removed and the space freed.
- `forge_pr_view` (and `cel forge pr view|list`) now report `closed_at` on both forges.
