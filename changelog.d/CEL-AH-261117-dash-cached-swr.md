### Fixed
- The dashboard's upstream caches (agents, inbox, PRs, decisions and the rest) are stale-while-revalidate: past a cache's budget an ask gets the last good answer at once while one refresh runs behind it, so `/api/state` no longer waits on `cel sessions --json` every 8 s. Only the first ask waits; a failed or throwing refresh keeps the last good answer. An answer older than ten times its budget (after a long idle) is not served at once: that ask waits for a fresh read.
- Dashboard writes act on fresh reads, never a warm answer: answering or dropping a decision re-reads the decisions list first, and every v2 action re-reads the roster, workspaces and drive list it checks against. A restart from the dashboard forgets the roster, so the next view shows who is running after it.
