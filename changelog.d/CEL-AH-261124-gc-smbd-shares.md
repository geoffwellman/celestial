### Fixed
- `cel gc` no longer keeps every worktree and review checkout because an own-uid Samba server (`smbd`, serving a share to the owner) has an unreadable cwd and fd table. Both the worktree live check and the review-checkout pass now treat such an `smbd` as occupying every directory Samba exports (from `testparm -s`, falling back to `smb.conf` and its includes, plus `net usershare` exports; `[homes]` is the passwd home; symlinks resolved): a worktree or checkout inside, equal to, or containing a share is kept, and nothing else is. The live check right before a removal reads the shares afresh. When the shares cannot be determined (an unparsable config line, an unreadable usershare), everything is still kept.
