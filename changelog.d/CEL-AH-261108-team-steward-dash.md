### Added
- The steward nudges a solo product's agent when the verdict recorded with `cel solo review` (`.cel/solo-reviews.json`) is `changes` at the PR's current head, once per head: a push stays quiet until the reviewer records the new head, and a `changes` verdict on the new head nudges again. PR heads come from the forge layer (`forge_pr_list`, GitHub or Forgejo). It is silenced exactly like the review sweep - an open owner card linked to the PR (`cel decide ask --pr`) or a `hold`/`superseded` label - and a GitHub PR whose own reviews already request changes at that head is left to the existing reviewDecision nudge, so the finding is said once. The forge is asked only about verdicts that could still be news: a head already nudged, or under an owner card, costs no call, and a verdict whose PR is closed, moved on or set aside is looked at again after a day.
- Forge PR objects (`forge_pr_view`, `forge_pr_list`, `cel forge pr view --json`) carry `labels` (names) on GitHub and Forgejo.
- Feature-team sweeps in the steward, reading only `.cel/teams/<feature>/team.json`, `events.jsonl` and `verdicts.json`: the lead is told once when a member has been `reserved` for over 10 minutes (launch never confirmed; keyed on the reservation) and once per candidate head when a specialist's latest verdict is for an older head than the repo's latest `candidate.updated`. Nothing is retired, relaunched or closed. A `team.json` or `verdicts.json` that does not parse, or lacks the fields read, is raised to root once per change and cleared when it reads again or the team is removed.
- A Feature teams card on dashboard v2 (`/api/v2/teams`, in the snapshot): per team its product, lead and state, each member's role/focus/state and whether an agent of exactly its alias is live, the candidate head per repo, each role's latest verdict marked fresh or stale against the candidate, and who owes the next move - from the team files alone, with no ledger and no branch inference. A team whose files do not read is shown as such.
