### Added
- `cel-fanout replace bind` (orchestrator-only): after `replace attach`, binds a replacement child's source checkout to the clean candidate's worktree without restarting the actor or touching the old (possibly dirty) tree. The actor is identified independently through herdr (name, pane, native session, cwd), the clean checkout must be a linked worktree of the same repository on the child branch at exactly the reserved head with a clean status, old review/gate evidence is superseded, and collect, the verifier, review and land refuse when the checkout or actor no longer matches the binding. One atomic write under a non-waiting lock; replay is a no-op; `--dry-run` writes nothing. gc keeps vetoing the retained old checkout.
