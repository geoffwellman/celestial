### Fixed
- When a fresh subscription read fails (the provider is down or rate-limited), windows from the last reading that have not reset are no longer dropped. Before this, an account whose week was spent at 100% looked fine until the next successful read. Each such window now keeps its last percentage, marked as last known (`stale: true`), together with the time it was read (`read_at`), until a reading replaces it. Windows that have reset since still show `reset, no fresh reading`. `cel quota` shows `7d 100% (last known, read 13:06; resets Fri 05:59)` and the dashboard Usage card shows `last known, read ...`. The steward keeps alerting on such a window (`... spent its 7d window (100%, last known reading at 13:06 ...)`), and `usage-pace` quotes it as `at the last known reading`. A 5h window still at 100% at its last known reading continues to veto the profiles routed at it.
- The dashboard Usage card now reads a reset time with no zone as UTC, matching how `cel quota` and the steward read it, and reads a numeric reset as epoch seconds (or milliseconds when the value is that large). Before this, a dashboard running east of UTC could show a window that was still running as already reset.
- Human times in `cel quota` and on the Usage card now include the day for any time more than a day away, whether it is in the past or the future.
