### Fixed
- Rex reviewer retirement recognises Pi 1.5's footer. Pi now prints the checkout's git branch after its cwd (`~/.local/state/cel/reviews/<repo>-pr-<n> (<branch>)`), so `tools/reviewers/retire.mjs` refused every idle Pi reviewer with `unknown_pi_footer` and `cel solo land`/`cel gc` never retired one - a merged PR's reviewer sat idle for over a day and held up a managed Rex upgrade. The footer's first line must still be this reviewer's own checkout (absolute or `~` form), now optionally followed by ` (<branch>)` and ` • <session name>`; another or longer path, a cut-off line or anything else after the branch is still `unknown_pi_footer`. The stats line is recognised by Pi's context-usage field (`23.4%/200k`, `?/200k`) instead of a parenthesised word, which a single-provider pane with auto-compaction off never showed, and `• thinking off` on that line no longer reads as an unsettled terminal. Fixture: `tools/reviewers/fixtures/pi-1.5.0-idle-footer.txt`, from a live idle Pi 1.5.0 pane.

### Changed
- `cel gc`'s retry of a Rex reviewer it cannot retire is counted and reported. Every sweep already tried again, but a refusal that does not clear by itself (like the footer above) only ever printed a warning to the steward's log. Each `unknown` answer is now recorded (`~/.local/state/cel/gc-reviewer-retire.json`, keyed by pane and PR; `cel solo land`'s attempt after its merge is the first), and at the sixth attempt (`CEL_GC_RETIRE_ALERT_ATTEMPTS`) or a day after the first, the product agent that owns the repo gets one `blocked` message naming the reviewer and the reason; a send that fails is retried next sweep. A retired, gone or protected answer ends the count. `cel solo land` now says what will happen: "`cel gc` retries it every tick and tells <product>-orch if it keeps failing".
