### Fixed
- The OpenCode launcher now waits up to 45s for the guard's location boot (the first location-scoped request, which loads every plugin) instead of the 2s every other request is capped at. After a Rex upgrade, with every agent relaunching at once, that request outlasted 2s and the launch refused with `plugin_request_timeout` (3 Nov, 4 Nov, 17 Nov) even though the same launch worked a minute later. The wait is still bounded and fails closed (no TUI), and `cel run --restart` now waits up to 100s (`CEL_REX_ADMIT_POLLS`, 1000 polls) for the launcher to admit a TUI.
- After a Rex restart or managed upgrade, a resume that only ran out of time (an OpenCode launcher refusing on one of its own timeouts) is retried once after `CEL_REX_RESUME_RETRY_SECS` (60s), unless the agent is live again by then or its retirement has begun. A retry that also fails is reported once and says it was retried. Other refusals (guard, auth, scope, session, vetoes, duplicates) are still reported without a retry. The restore now also waits for a resumed OpenCode launcher to admit its TUI or exit, closes the terminal of one that exited, and reports its refusal line instead of "it started, then exited".
- The resume retry is never run when the first attempt's dead terminal could not be closed (the failure names that terminal), when the agent was relaunched or stopped during the wait, or when its resume would now be vetoed (its saved session changed). Each restore pass retries at most `CEL_REX_RESUME_RETRY_MAX` (3) agents within `CEL_REX_RESUME_RETRY_BUDGET_SECS` (300s); any past that cap are reported as failed with "retry skipped: restore time cap". Worst case, retries add about 3 minutes to a steward tick, at most about 7. `rex_launch_settle` now re-reads the receipt's pid on every poll.
