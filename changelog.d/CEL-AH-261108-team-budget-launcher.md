### Added
- One box-wide live agent budget (`lib/budget.sh`, `cel budget [--json]`): live Celestial agents counted from the native inventory (herdr + Rex) by role - product agents, reviewers, team members, core/root - against `budget.max_live_agents` (default 8) and `budget.max_team_members` (default 4) in `~/.local/share/cel/config.yaml`, plus the memory floor. An unreadable inventory refuses (fail closed). A new `cel run reviewer` is admitted against it; product agents are counted and only warned, never blocked.
- `cel run member --workspace w --team <feature> --member <alias>`: the scoped-role launcher behind `cel team add`. It starts nothing without a one-time launch token (`<ws>/.cel/teams/<feature>/launch/<alias>.token`, spent before any check), launches only a `reserved` member row, admits it against the team budget, gives a builder its own worktree on `team/<feature>/<name>` at the recorded base, starts every other role in the team directory, sets `CEL_ROLE=member`, `CEL_TEAM`, `CEL_TEAM_ROLE`, `CEL_TEAM_MEMBER` and the alias mailbox, resolves `role_profiles.team-<role>` then `role_profiles.member`, places the pane in one `team <feature>` tab, and reports success only once the inventory shows that exact agent in that pane (exit 3 when unconfirmed). `member_retire` closes a member's pane only after proving its launch identity from the process, keeping a dirty or unpushed builder worktree.
- Guard rules for team members: a builder commits and pushes only `team/<feature>/*` and opens no PR; every other role reads repositories and writes only under its team directory, through `cel serve` and `cel team event|verdict|ack|status`; no member merges, lands, starts agents or panes, reads a token or writes to a forge by hand. No agent may run `cel run member` or touch a team launch token; the product agent runs `cel team ...`.
