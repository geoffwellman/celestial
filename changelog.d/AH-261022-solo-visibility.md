### Added
- Solo products are visible everywhere the fleet is. `cel fleet --json` units carry `mode` and `solo_worktrees`, and the text view draws a solo product as `solo <agent state>  worktrees N` instead of worker counts. The dashboard tags a solo product's agent row "solo".
- The steward tells a solo product's agent, once per head (not once per four-hour window), when one of its PRs would pass `cel solo land` right now - the same rules, including `merge: self` and every protected check green on the head - and is still unmerged. It never lands anything itself.
- `cel gc` removes a solo agent's own worktrees (`<ws>/.cel/solo/<product>/<branch>`, kept also after a product switches back to fleet) through the same checks as orphaned worker worktrees - no live process, nothing unsaved or unpushed, no `--force` - and only on a MERGED or CLOSED PR whose head is exactly the worktree's HEAD, so a reused branch name never inherits an old merge. One with no such PR is kept (`no-pr`).
