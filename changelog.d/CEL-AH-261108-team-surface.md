### Added

- Feature teams: `cel team start|add|ack|event|verdict|status|retire|close` (lib/team.sh). A product's agent builds small work itself and, for a feature that needs it, leads a team kept in `<ws>/.cel/teams/<feature>/` (team.json, brief.md, acceptance.yaml, contracts/, events.jsonl, verdicts.json, per-member scope packets). Every write holds the folder's lock and calls nothing external while holding it.
- `cel team add` admits each member against the box-wide agent budget before writing anything, reserves its row, writes its scope packet and a one-time launch token, and launches it with `cel run member`; it becomes ready only once the launch is confirmed and the member ran `cel team ack`. An unconfirmed launch stays reserved and is never relaunched. Builder `--owns` globs may not overlap another live builder's in the same repo; an overlap that cannot be decided exactly is refused with the reason.
- Verdicts are bound to a full head and show as stale in `cel team status` once the lead posts a newer candidate; `cel team close` refuses while a builder's work is not on origin unless `--force-keep`.
- Team role files `core/roles/team-{product,design,builder,qa,platform,release,analyst}.md` and focus packs under `core/roles/focus/<role>/`. The solo role now carries the team-lead guidance (when to form a team, waves of at most four, the integration-branch rule, hand-off, owner decisions); `project-orchestrator.md` points at it.
