### Fixed
- A steward `cleared:` line is said once per change. Taking a condition down now resolves every open item under its key in one pass, under one line; before, a mailbox holding several open items for one key (written before roll-up) lost one per tick and said `cleared:` every tick until the last was gone - root saw "stale launch differences ... wait on open owner decisions" every five minutes while the owner card stayed open. The line goes out only when an item was actually resolved, so a failed resolve leaves it open without claiming it cleared. A difference that comes back afterwards is raised again and cleared again with one more line. This applies to every steward clear (launch lines, orchestrator starts, quota, pools, services, Rex upgrades, teams and the rest).
- A freed suite lock says `cleared:` once per workspace that was told, instead of a second line beside the clear.
- A newly blocked Rex version takes down every open blocked item from an older version, so its word opens a fresh item instead of landing as an update on an old one.
