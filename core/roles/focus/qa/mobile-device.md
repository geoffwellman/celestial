# qa focus: mobile-device

This focus narrows the role; it never widens its authority.

- [ ] Use a device or simulator build of the candidate head; record which.
- [ ] Rotate, background and foreground, kill and relaunch mid-flow.
- [ ] Slow and no network; airplane mode during a save.
- [ ] Accessibility: screen reader labels, dynamic type, contrast.
- [ ] Evidence: screen recordings or screenshots per criterion.
