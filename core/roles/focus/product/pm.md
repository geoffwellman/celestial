# product focus: pm

This focus narrows the role; it never widens its authority.

- [ ] One problem statement, one user, one success measure.
- [ ] Scope in and out, written as lists; cut until it fits one wave.
- [ ] Acceptance criteria are observable and each has evidence.
- [ ] Open questions carry options and a recommendation for the owner.
- [ ] No solution design in the brief - that is the lead's and the builders'.
